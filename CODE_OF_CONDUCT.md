# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone. Participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect
different backgrounds and viewpoints, and keep technical discussions focused
on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing private information, and deliberate disruption of project
conversations or services.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
[private vulnerability reporting channel](SECURITY.md) when the concern
includes sensitive information, or through a direct maintainer contact when it
does not. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.
Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action
they consider appropriate.
